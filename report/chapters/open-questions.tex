\section{What the Results Leave Unexplained}
\label{sec:unexplained}

The record marks the following as open or unanalysed; this report adds nothing to
them.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Why the chosen die-slip set avoids the cup loop that one of its fixes opened
  on one scene is open (\BF{59}).
  \item The slow tail of episodes, replan loops of observe, replan, observe, is a
  finding of its own that none of the four time cuts addresses and the record does
  not analyse (\BF{53}).
  \item The die under a cup is found only by lifting the cup; whether the plan lifts
  that cup depends on a region behind it that the die is not in (\BF{65}).
  \item The shortfall of LW1's planner calls and lengths at 5x5 against its paper's
  Table 2 \cite{bonet2014flexible} has a likely cause, the code version, that no one has checked with the
  authors (\TK{4}).
  \item TAMPURA's time per episode in our runs is shorter than the product of its
  published per-step time and step count; the audit file notes a candidate reason, that
  the paper's per-step time includes 200 samples of TAMPURA's effects function, and
  confirms none (\PW{4}, \Cref{sec:published-fd}).
  \item Whether TAMPURA's three look loops on our scenes come from the fixed camera is
  open: the hand-camera batch was cut at 13:48 after two seeds, neither of which looped
  (\TK{5}).
  \item Whether the Semantic Boxels system can conclude where it is safe to go in the
  Wumpus world is the question of path 10; its runs have not produced a result
  (\TK{10}).
\end{itemize}
