\chapter{Deviations from the Published Tasks and Setups}
\label{ch:appendix-deviations}

A deviation is anything in our setup that differs from the published setup for the
same task: TAMPURA's own, or LW1's for path 4. Under rule 12 of the tracks every deviation is approved by Hani in
writing in chat before anything that rests on it is run or merged. The last column
gives whom the source says the deviation favours: T for TAMPURA, ours for our arm,
or neither; ``not stated'' where the source gives no direction. The lists are as of
7 November, between about 13:00 and 14:30, with Hani's answers of 8 November added.
Approvals are as the tracks record them.
Paths 4 and 5 and the rows C1 to C17 of path 6 rest on Hani's earlier written answers,
which rule 12 counts as approved: for path 5 those of 6 November at 16:25 and 7
November at about 10:55, for path 4 those of 6 November at 15:08 and 16:25. The table
gives a time only where the track states one.

\section{Seeds and Scenes That Never Became an Episode}
\label{sec:rejections}

Some planned seeds and scenes never become a scored episode. \Cref{tab:rejections} gives
every kind on record, read from the raw files of each evaluation and from the code that
applies the criterion (trunk of 15 November; \ID{rejections}{rejection record}). Two rules
of 14 November decide the count. A scene that never exists for any system (our scene
builder cannot build it, the same for every arm) stays out of the success rate and is
named beside it. A run that started and then failed, crashed, hung, was cut by our own
side, or received another run's record is a failure of that arm, with its cause named; a
re-rolled replacement, a rerun or a retry never counts. No record shows TAMPURA's own
scene generator rejecting a seed: every rejection on record is made by our scene builder
or our harness.
