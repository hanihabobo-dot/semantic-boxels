\begin{summary}
Since the thesis \cite{alassiri2026thesis} was handed in on 10 June 2026 the work has
removed its simplifications, put the system into the environment of TAMPURA
\cite{curtis2024partially}, and opened a set of parallel tracks. Six points sum up
the state of 7 November.

\emph{The system no longer cheats in grasping and perception.} The weld that held
every object is gone and the gripper holds by friction; detection reads rendered
images instead of the simulator's exact poses; failures of the physics are
detected and reported as failures (\PW{1}, \PW{2}). Thirty-two field findings were
fixed or recorded on the way (\PW{1}).

\emph{The comparison with TAMPURA now runs inside its environment, with our own
perception, belief, planner and physical execution.} On the three scenes that
TAMPURA ships, the pipeline with four die-slip fixes solved 120 of 120 episodes over
two jobs, against 209 of 300 without them over five jobs and 40 of 60 for TAMPURA
(\BF{59}). On 40 scenes from TAMPURA's own generator, ours solved 57 of 80 and
TAMPURA 46 of 76, with pairs 18 to 9 and exact McNemar $p = 0.12$: ours is ahead and
the lead is not significant (\BF{63}). Our run of TAMPURA lands within 1.4
standard errors of its published return: a mean discounted return of 0.530
against the paper's 0.63 $\pm$ 0.07 (\Cref{sec:published-fd}).

\emph{Our occlusion model does not model where the die hides on TAMPURA's task.}
The die lies in none of our hiding regions, which begin at the back face of the
object that casts them; we find it only by lifting the cup over it, which we do
because the space behind the cup is unknown. The paper must not claim more
(\BF{65}).

\emph{The classical planner LW1 drives our robot in a 3-D Wumpus world.} It won 18
of 18 episodes with our perception as its observations, none of the 369 percepts
wrong; LW1 alone makes fewer planner calls and shorter runs than its paper's
Table 2 at 5x5 (\TK{4}). A straight-line walk wins 18 and 15 of 100 of LW1's
states at 5x5, a walker that never refuses an unknown cell 34, so the test has a luck
floor of about a third; no published number for these walkers appears in the papers
read for this report (\TK{10}, \Cref{sec:published}). Our own planner has no result
there yet and must clear this floor, not zero (\Cref{tab:wumpus-bar}).

\emph{The thesis's ``timeout'' share on stack needs correcting.} Of 29 rows
labelled ``timeout'' in the thesis sweep's semantic stack cells, 25 ended after 8
to 21 seconds, a symbolic success that the end-of-run physics check rejected
(\BF{68}).

\emph{Twelve dev tracks run from a stable base.} Our planning layer on TAMPURA's
own execution reaches their reward in 18 of 20 \cuncert\ episodes and 17 of 25
\slamcol\ episodes (\TK{2}); TAMPURA's planner on our scenes solves 6 of 10 where
ours solves 10 of 10 (\TK{5}); no dev track has run anything on the cluster, and a
list of decisions waits for Hani (\Cref{ch:open-questions}). Hani's visual check under
rule 15 is on record for one run and for no approved comparison
(\Cref{sec:visual-check}).
\end{summary}
