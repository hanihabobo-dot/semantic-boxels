\begin{preface}
This is version 2.1 of an internal report on the Semantic Boxels project since the
thesis; version 2 was its final version for Checkpoint 1. On 8 November 2026 Hani named the state
of that day a checkpoint: the dev tracks pause there, and the project is presented to the
supervisor. The report states the work as of that day, the date on the title page; where
the time of a file matters, the section gives it. Versions 1, 1.1 and 1.2 came out on 7
and 8 November (version 1 is commit 98294ff). Version 2 brings every track to the
checkpoint (\Cref{tab:tracks}) and adds the findings of 8 November: the drops of tall
boxes (\Cref{sec:path3}), the look-at candidates of path 9, the WSL wedge (\BF{69}), the
amendment of the cluster evaluation (\BF{70}) and the leak sweep, with rules 17 and 18
and Hani's answers of that day. The summary now stands alone. Version 2.1, of 14
November, changes nothing else of version 2: on Hani's decisions after the review of
the code (\ID{review}{code review}), every place that sets our arm against TAMPURA
carries one fixed validity note, every number that compares the arms counts both over
the same episodes, the first attempt of each, a crash as a failure and the number of
crashed runs named, and \Cref{sec:comparable} says how
the code counts drops and collisions.

The report was written by a Claude session (path 11 of the dev tracks of
\BF{68}) from the project's own record: the audit file, the documents of the dev
tracks and the thesis. Each number of ours in the text was copied from the entry or file
that it names, and carries a tag that links to its row in
\appref{ch:appendix-sources}. Outside work is cited from the bibliography of the thesis
wherever it has an entry there. The five entries added to it, and every published number
in \Cref{sec:published-fd} and \Cref{sec:published}, were checked in the papers
themselves; \ID{citations}{the citation record} gives for each the place and the date.

Three checks were run on version 2 before it was delivered. The logs of the build show
no undefined reference or citation. A script lists every citation key of the text, finds
it in one of the two bibliography files, and finds a row of
\appref{ch:appendix-sources} for every source tag. And one verifier agent compared the
text changed since version 1 (all but this preface), most of the report, with the entries and files it
was taken from. Of about 520 items it found 11 that its source contradicts, 8 that no source supports and 5 that later records had overtaken, and could not recheck 2. The text was corrected for the 24 problems; \ID{verify}{the verification records} give every row.
Version 1 had had the same check on all of its text: its verifier checked 492
items, found 29 that its source contradicts, 7 that no source supports and 1 number
without a tag, and the text was corrected.

Two limits of the report are worth knowing before reading. The tracks paused at
Checkpoint 1, so the state of a track is the one of that checkpoint, with a few later
facts given with their time, and the files of the tracks change when the next round
starts. And where the record disagrees with itself, \Cref{sec:disagreements} says which
figure the report uses.
\end{preface}
