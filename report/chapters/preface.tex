\begin{preface}
This is version 1.2 of an internal report on the Semantic Boxels project since the
thesis. It states the work as of 7 November 2026, the date on the title page, with a
few later facts given with their date. Versions 1 (commit 98294ff) and 1.1 came out on
7 November; 1.1 added the Wumpus luck floor beside LW1 and our predictions
(\Cref{tab:wumpus-bar}) and the visual check per comparison (\Cref{sec:visual-check}).
Version 1.2 adds facts of 8 November: the success criterion is under review
(\Cref{sec:catalogue}), rule 14 is lifted, and a second watch run of Hani's is on
record. Later versions give their date in the same place and say here what changed.

The report was written by a Claude session (path 11 of the dev tracks of
\BF{68}) from the project's own record: the audit file, the documents of the dev
tracks and the thesis. Six read-only agents first condensed the record into working
notes with line numbers, which are kept with the tracks' documents. Each number of
ours in the text was then copied from the entry or file that the notes name, and
carries a tag that links to its row in \appref{ch:appendix-sources}. Outside work is
cited from the bibliography of the thesis wherever it has an entry there. The five
entries added to it, and every published number in \Cref{sec:published-fd} and
\Cref{sec:published}, were checked in the papers themselves; \ID{citations}{the citation record}
gives for each the place and the date.

Three checks were run on version 1 before it was delivered. The logs of the build
show no undefined reference or citation. A script lists every citation key of the text,
finds it in one of the two bibliography files, and finds a row of
\appref{ch:appendix-sources} for every source tag. And one verifier agent compared the
five added bibliography entries with their sources, and the numbers, quotations and
dated statements of the text with the entries and files they were taken from. It
checked 492 items. It found 29 that its source contradicts, 7 that no source supports
and 1 number without a tag; it marked 23 statements about the tracks as overtaken by
later changes, and it could not check 5. The text was corrected for the first three
groups and for most of the fourth, and \ID{verify}{the verification record} lists what was
left unchecked. The verifier did not read the corrected text again, and the checks of
the build and the script were run again after the corrections. They were run again
for versions 1.1 and 1.2, whose additions the writer compared with the files they
cite; no verifier read them.

Two limits of the report are worth knowing before reading. The tracks are live, so
the state of a track is as of the time named in its section, mostly between about 13:05
and 14:30 on 7 November, with a few later facts given with their time; what came after,
such as the thirteenth path that Go started at 16:02, is left for the next version
(\ID{tracksstate}{tracks state file}). And where the record disagrees with itself,
\Cref{sec:disagreements} says which figure the report uses.
\end{preface}
