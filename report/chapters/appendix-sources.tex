\chapter{Internal Sources}
\label{ch:appendix-sources}

Every tag of the text links to its row here. A row of the form BF\#$n$ names the
entry of the audit file \texttt{PAPER\_AUDIT.txt} (at the root of the repository)
with that number; \#P$n$ names the workstream entry; ``path $n$'' names the dev
track of that number, whose files are under \texttt{tracks/} in the evidence
folder. The evidence folders are under
\texttt{archive/tampura\_bridge\_plan\_2026-10-25/} unless a full path is given;
``the entry'' means that the entry's own text is the evidence. Two numbers, BF\#61
and BF\#67, have no entry of their own; their rows say where they are named. The
status column
gives the marker in the entry's heading as written; where later text of the entry or
a later entry changes it, \Cref{sec:disagreements} says so.
