D2  HARNESS: BoxelBridgePolicy (instantiated directly) returns ONE action per

    env.problem_spec.action_schemas (no preconditions, identity effects_fn,
    OUR execute_fn).  Their rollout, their env.step, their Belief.update and
    their get_reward run untouched: our execute_fn performs D9's set-up and
    settle, builds the env adapter, takes the first render, boxelizes and runs

    returns the SceneState it received and the D3 observation; their update
    applies the observation; their reward judges it; steps 1..19 return no-op
    (their no-op branch deep-copies the belief, so a reward reached at step 0
    persists to rewards[-1] without any bridge write).  The info dict holds
    only JSON-able values; our ledger lives in our files.  REJECTED: the
    archived no-op policy that mutated their SceneBelief in place
    (belief_bridge.py:44-58); it bypassed their observation channel and is
    where the forced success lived.

D3  SCORING CHANNEL: tampura_bridge/scoring.py ObservationAdapter builds the
    terminal observation from physical readback only:
    SceneObservation(camera_image=None, poses={}, grasp=G, grasp_body=S,
    conf=Q).  S is the store symbol s in env.sim_world.objects with
    store.get(s) == the body id that both finger pads contact with squeeze
    force >= 0.5 N (the harness's _verify_holding rule) and no constraint on
    that body, resolved with the same lookup their obs_from_camera_image uses
    (env.py:70-73); never chosen by name; None when the pads hold nothing or a
    non-task body.  G is a store symbol of type grasp added once through
    store.add_typed(<plain tuple: position, quaternion, width>, 'grasp') when
    S is not None, so deep copies and the pickle stay plain data.  Q is the
    real readback of the 7 arm joints, never a constant.  camera_image and
    poses stay empty: the reward does not read them, and a render through
