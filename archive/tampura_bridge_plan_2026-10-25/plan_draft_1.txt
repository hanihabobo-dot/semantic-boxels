    reads (true poses, body-id matching, TAMPURA's belief or world internals)

    no planner swap, no plan post-processing, no hardcoded action sequence, no

    never worked around.

    the user and the paper carries no dashes.

P4.2  GROUND TRUTH IN ONE PAGE (verified 2026-10-25 against the mirrored source)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THEIR TASK.  find_dice: a Franka Panda (models/srl/franka_panda/panda.urdf,
tool link panda_tool_tip, wrist camera link camera_frame, 640x480, fx=fy=525),
a table whose top is z=0, one YCB die plus 1 to 5 upside-down YCB cups
(a..e_cups_ud) at x in [0.3,0.7], y in [-0.4,0.4].  Exactly THREE scene files
exist (problems/1747173590 = die + 4 cups, 1747173591 = die + 1 cup,
1747173593 = die + 4 cups); get_scene_data picks one with random.choice after
the global seed (env.py:801-811).  The die sits at the hiding cup's XY, inside
the inverted cup; in two files it floats at cup-centre height (z 0.049, 0.052)
because their world never steps physics; in the third it rests on the table
(z 0.021) 1.5 cm from the cup centre.  Scenes were generated so that the die is
absent from the wrist camera's segmentation at DEFAULT_ARM_POS
(env_generator.py:164-180).  Pinned seed-to-scene map of the 2026-05-26 run
(from each console's "Loading scene from" line; os.listdir order dependent):
  1747173590 <- seeds 1,2,3,4,8,14,15,18      (TAMPURA 2/8)
  1747173591 <- seeds 0,7,9,11,12,13,16       (TAMPURA 6/7)
  1747173593 <- seeds 5,6,10,17,19            (TAMPURA 3/5)
Actions: pick(o,g) [requires not moved(o)], place(o,g,p), look(o1,o2,q)
["look behind o2 for o1", depends moved(o2)], go-home [only while holding],
no-op.  Streams sampled ONCE at t=0 (5 grasps from a saved per-mesh grasp
library, 5 placements uniform on a shortened table with no inter-object
collision check, 5 look confs 20-30 cm above each object's t=0 pose).  Their
belief: known poses plus a 15 mm visibility voxel grid; voxels inside a KNOWN
object's collision mesh are cleared, so the cavity of an inverted cup stays a
die candidate: their containment model uses the known category mesh.  Their
perception hands out the TRUE pose of every body whose id is in the seg mask
(env.py:57-75).  Execution is kinematic: headless trajectories TELEPORT to the
last waypoint, the carried object follows the tool by resetBasePositionAnd-
Orientation each step, stepSimulation is never called, picks cannot physically
fail, nothing falls.  Reward = Exists o: is-target(o) and holding(o), and
at-home (conf within 1e-4 of DEFAULT_ARM_POS), evaluated on the abstract
BELIEF; holding enters the belief only when their pick_execute_fn's IK + BiRRT
succeeded; go-home's observation reports DEFAULT_ARM_POS even when its motion
plan failed (env.py:563-581).  Rollout: exactly max_steps = 20 steps, no early
stop, no-op after the goal; loop_s = time_deltas[-1] covers get_action (200
outcome samples per replan, each an IK/BiRRT/render; SymK itself 0.04-1.5 s per
episode) plus env.step.  Local run 2026-05-26: 11/20, loop 240 +/- 151 s over
all episodes, 166 +/- 85 s over successes; all inspected failures are the same
degeneracy (a look that reveals nothing is re-selected to step 20 under the
envelope rule, tampura_policy.py:83-99).  The policy hook: a Policy subclass
with get_action(belief, store) -> (Action(name, store symbols), info, store),
registered by tampura.config.config.register_planner; env.step(action, b,
store) dispatches to the action schema's execute_fn(action, belief, env.state,
store) -> (state, observation), then b.update(action, observation, store).

OUR MODEL.  Belief = BeliefState status per fragment (unknown / not_here /
found / unresolved) + one Know-If fluent obj_at_boxel_KIF.  sense(?o ?region)
needs handempty, view_clear(region), not KIF, boxel_fits; it optimistically
asserts the target in the region; view_clear is derived from static
blocks_view_at facts and the blockers' current boxels.  Sense takes no
configuration: the camera is fixed (methods.tex:170).  Shadows are 2.5-D
lateral volumes cast from the fixed camera; the footprint of an object is never
inside a shadow (shadow_calculator.py:217-236).  Everything downstream of
shadow casting (belief, census, corridor facts, sense classifier, shrink,
retire, discovery) is generic over "a SHADOW-typed AABB with a caster id";
nothing requires the fragment to lie behind its caster.  Execution is physical
since #P1: friction grasp with tactile verification, motor release, integrity
checks; the one exempt hardcoded motion is the post-pick / post-place lift
(0.10 m in code, execution.py:869, the audit text says ~5 cm; corrected in
BF#1 below).  Constants tied to our setup: Panda links 9/10/11 hardcoded in
five files, external camera at (0.1,-0.8,0.7), table top 0.325, reach gate
R 0.815 about a WORLD z of 0.066, GRASP_MAX_SPAN 0.076, class priors 0.04 /
0.03 and sense-grid offsets derived from 3-4 cm cubes.  execution.py and the
robot_utils motion functions address pybullet's DEFAULT client.

THE GAP.  Their die is hidden by CONTAINMENT (inside a cup, same XY); our
shadows are lateral and never contain it.  Their camera moves; ours is fixed.
Their goal needs at-home; ours stops at holding.  Their execution is
kinematic; ours is physical.  Their 20 seeds are 20 stochastic trials over
three layouts.

P4.3  DESIGN DECISIONS (D1-D9; each is a BF# entry once implemented)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  WHAT RUNS: our WHOLE pipeline (main loop, PDDLStream planner, streams,
    render-based perception, physical execution) inside TAMPURA's executing
    PyBullet world, on their seeds, through their rollout.  Not a planner-only
    port onto their kinematic controllers (that variant is B9, optional, after
    the headline campaign, and would be labelled "planners under TAMPURA's
    executor").  Rationale: #P4's title and the user's words ("let our setup
    work on their problem"); the thesis claims concern the whole method.

D2  HARNESS: our policy returns ONE action per episode, a schema named
    boxel_episode appended at runtime to env.problem_spec.action_schemas (no
    preconditions, identity effects_fn, OUR execute_fn).  Their rollout, their
    env.step, their Belief.update and their get_reward run untouched: our
    execute_fn runs the entire plan-act-sense loop (bounded by the episode wall
    cap) and returns (state, observation); their update applies the
    observation; their reward judges it; steps 1..19 return no-op.  Their
    time_deltas therefore covers our whole episode (G18).  REJECTED
    alternative: the archived no-op policy that mutated their SceneBelief in
    place (belief_bridge.py:44-58): it bypassed their observation channel and
    is where the forced success lived.  The schema registration is the single
    runtime addition G13 allows, and it is a data registration through their
    own extension point, not an edit.

D3  SCORING CHANNEL: the observation our execute_fn returns is built by ONE
    class, tampura_bridge/scoring.py ObservationAdapter, from physical
    readback only: camera_image = a real render of their wrist camera at the
    final arm configuration, passed through THEIR obs_from_camera_image (their
    perception semantics, used for scoring only); grasp / grasp_body = set iff
    both finger pads report contact with one body, squeeze force >= 0.5 N
    (the harness's own _verify_holding rule), and p.getNumConstraints() == 0
    for that body, with grasp_body = the contacted body's id (never the
    target's name); conf = the real joint readback.  Nothing else in the bridge
    may touch their belief (G6).  TAMPURA's own go-home reports at-home even
    on a failed motion plan; ours cannot, so our arm is scored STRICTER than
    theirs on this point; this asymmetry is disclosed in the paper rather than
    imitated (H1).

D4  THE FIXED CAMERA IS THEIR WRIST CAMERA AT DEFAULT_ARM_POS.  No external
    camera is added to their world (an external eye changes what "hidden"
    means relative to their generator's wrist-camera check and would make some
    seeds trivially visible).  Consequence: a sense is only valid with the arm
    at the sensing configuration, so the domain's sense action gains a
    configuration precondition (model tweak M1, P4.5) and the planner must
    plan a move to q_home before each sense.  This replaces the archived
    bridge's unplanned arm park (CB#120(g)) with a planned action, and it is
    the degenerate case of the sensing-configuration extension the thesis
    names as future work (conclusion.tex:27-33).

D5  CONTAINMENT = AN INTERIOR REGION CAST BY A COVERING OBJECT (model tweak
    M2, P4.5): a SHADOW-typed belief region spanning the covering object's
    footprint from the table to its estimated top, created_by that object,
    wired exactly like a lateral fragment (belief.add_shadow, shadow_occluder_
    map, census, corridor facts, sense over its grid).  The planner, the
    streams and the sense action are unchanged.  Which objects cover:
    objects whose CATEGORY is declared may_cover in the bridge task spec
    (a..e_cups_ud), the same category knowledge TAMPURA uses when it clears
    voxels inside known meshes.  REJECTED: a new region type with its own
    predicates (redundant with blocks_view_at + view_clear); a lift-and-look or
    sense-while-holding action (contradicts the handempty gate and the
    2026-08-21 field evidence, and reopens scripted motion); casting interior
    regions for every object (changes thesis-scene behaviour: a 4 cm cube
    would be worth looking under a 13 cm solid cube), kept available behind a
    task-spec switch but not used.  A solid cube's interior is occupied by the
    cube; hollowness is a class property and is declared as such.

D6  GOAL: ('and', ('holding', die), ('at_config', q_home)), matching their
    reward.  No new go_home action: with M1's init facts (Config q_home),
    (config_for_boxel q_home home), the existing move action plans the motion
    home through plan-motion with the held object as cargo.  goal_satisfied
    gains an at_config head verified against joint readback.

D7  TARGET-CLASS CONSTANTS ARE TASK-SPEC INPUTS, SET ONCE (B6): NOMINAL_HIDDEN_
    EXTENTS and _MIN, SENSE_LOW_SLICE_OFFSET, SENSE_DENSE_SPACING, the octree
    auto_cell, the reach envelope, the finger opening, derived from the
    declared die and cup class sizes measured on the YCB meshes (B1) and from
    their Panda's URDF, passed into the pipeline as parameters.  Our scenes
    keep their defaults.  A value changed after looking at a seed's outcome is
    solution encoding and aborts the package (G9).

D8  PHYSICS IN THEIR WORLD: our pipeline steps physics (friction grasp needs
    it).  At episode start the floating die of two scene files drops onto the
    table inside its cup (still hidden, same XY); if their task bodies are
    static (B1 verifies the masses), they are made dynamic ONCE at build time
    for all task bodies with declared masses, nothing else of theirs is
    altered (no friction raising: the archived bridge's 4.0 is forbidden).
    Both facts are BF# entries and paper footnotes.  TAMPURA's arm needs none
    of this (kinematic) and is run as shipped.

D9  BUDGETS: one wall cap of 900 s per episode on both arms (tampura_sweep.py's
    kill), our --max-plan-time set so no plan() outlives the episode; no action
    cap on our arm (their 20-step cap is their step semantics; our actions are
    finer grained; executed action counts are reported).  Pre-registered here,
    not adjusted after results.

P4.5  THE MODEL TWEAKS AND THEIR WORDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
M1  SENSING CONFIGURATION.
    BEFORE: sense(?o ?region) with preconditions handempty, view_clear,
    not KIF, boxel_fits; the camera is external and fixed.
    AFTER: sense(?o ?region ?q) adds (Config ?q) (at_config ?q)
    (sense_config ?q); sense_config is a static init fact; everything else
    identical.  Init adds (Config q_home) (sense_config q_home) (Boxel home)
    (config_for_boxel q_home home) so the existing move action can reach it.
    Our own scenes declare no sensing configuration and keep the live domain.
    POD JUSTIFICATION: the observation's precondition Pre(Y) now includes the
    sensing pose; the observation itself is unchanged (deterministic render
    over the region's grid).  This is the degenerate, single-configuration
    case of the robot-mounted-camera extension the thesis names
    (conclusion.tex:27-33).
    CLAIM EFFECT: methods.tex:170 ("sense calls no streams because the camera
    is fixed") stays true for the thesis scenes; the paper states that in the
    find_dice runs the fixed viewpoint is the wrist camera at the home
    configuration and that reaching it is a planned move.  The planner now
    pays a move before each sense: a cost, not a shortcut.

M2  INTERIOR REGIONS.
    BEFORE: hidden regions are lateral occlusion shadows only; an object's
    footprint is never hidden space.
    AFTER: for an object whose category is declared may_cover, the volume
    under its footprint (table to estimated top) is a hidden region of kind
    interior, cast by that object, searched by the same sense action after the
    object has been relocated and the arm is at the sensing configuration.
    Classes that are not declared cast nothing (all thesis scenes).
    POD JUSTIFICATION: a belief region with a Know-If literal, cleared only by
    an observation of its grid; discovery by relocation plus sensing; the
    thesis's own hiddenness definition (methods.tex:138, every corner's line
    of sight blocked) already covers it; conclusion.tex:41-42 names interiors
    of concave geometry as the intended next region type.
    CLAIM EFFECT: the "Boxel types" vocabulary gains a region kind; no claim
    about lateral shadows changes; results.tex:352 (TAMPURA keeps occlusion
    sub-symbolic, we name it) becomes stronger, since the interior is now a
    named inspectable region on our side too.  The parity point to state: our
    class declaration is the same information TAMPURA takes from the known
    mesh when it clears voxels inside it.

M3  GOAL CONJUNCT at_config(q_home): a goal form, not a domain change; the
    paper states the goal is holding the die at home for both systems.

M4  SCORING: success for both systems = TAMPURA's reward on TAMPURA's belief
    through TAMPURA's observation channel (D2, D3).  Our arm's belief inputs
    come from physical readback; theirs from their kinematic executor.  The
    paper states both.

CORRECTIONS THE PAPER NEEDS REGARDLESS: the 3-strike give-up now parks a
fragment unresolved and never asserts it empty (execution.py:3061-3079; the
thesis text says "marked as not containing the target"); the post-action lift
is 0.10 m; SymK is an FD top-k fork, so "the same Fast Downward-based planner"
means the FD lineage; derived predicates exist in our domain, so "no deductive
axioms" must read "no LW1-style knowledge axioms over K-literals".
