OUR MODEL.  Belief = BeliefState status per fragment (unknown / not_here /
found / unresolved) plus one Know-If fluent obj_at_boxel_KIF.  sense(?o
?region) needs handempty, view_clear(region), not KIF, boxel_fits; it
optimistically asserts the target in the region; view_clear is derived from
static blocks_view_at facts and the blockers' current boxels.  Sense takes no
configuration: the camera is fixed (methods.tex:170).  Shadows are 2.5-D
lateral volumes cast from the fixed camera from registered task bodies only,
by rayTestBatch on the default client: none of the three cast sites passes a
client (boxel_env.py:1512-1513; execution.py:2356-2357, 2979-2980), so in our
scenes a ray can stop at a body not yet registered (PAPER_AUDIT item (h));
an object's footprint is never inside a shadow (shadow_calculator.py:217-236);
the robot is excluded from detection (boxel_env.py:1749-1752) and casts
nothing.  Free space is the workspace box minus registered OBJECT and SHADOW
AABBs (free_space.py:47-48, 86-102), and the planner gets KIF for the target
on every OBJECT and free boxel on audit #67's premise that the camera covers
the whole table (pddlstream_planner.py:667-681).  After a place the moved
object's box is re-posed from the render and no new shadow is cast
(test_full_pipeline.py:1910-1975, matching methods.tex:157); new fragments are
cast only when a sense discovers or rediscovers a body (execution.py:2255-2384,
2948-3003; the rediscovery path first deletes the body's old fragments from the
registry and the belief without a render, :2871-2884, and carves the recast
only against OBJECT boxels, :2960-2980, while register_new_detections also
carves against existing fragments, :2278-2279; F9 bounds rediscovery to 3).
The deleted area becomes free space and receives the not-here literal without
an observation (pddlstream_planner.py:675-681): an unsound belief write in
today's pipeline (M7).  After a move the loop names the joint readback after
the target configuration whatever the residual (test_full_pipeline.py:
1591-1597), and RobotConfig equality is by name (streams.py:118-124).  The
goal is ('holding', target) (test_full_pipeline.py:762) and the loop ends as
soon as the target body is held (_loop_done, :1201-1209); init gives Config
and at_config only to current_config (pddlstream_planner.py:889-890); the
planner's home_config is REST_POSES under the symbol q_home (streams.py:
255-258).  The planner reads pddl/domain_pddlstream.pddl unconditionally
(pddlstream_planner.py:177).
Execution is physical since #P1: friction grasp with tactile verification,
motor release, integrity checks; the one exempt hardcoded motion is the

SUCCESS.  rewards[-1] == 1.0 from each arm's RolloutHistory (G12).  Secondary

HARNESS STOP RULE: three gate-failed episodes of one arm with the same hook
stop the campaign; the partial table is reported with the stop reason, and a
run after the fix is a new campaign with its own BF#, reported beside the
stopped one.
STATISTICS.  Primary evidence: the paired per-seed table of repetition 1
(seed, scene file, T outcome, O outcome, both times).  Primary test: McNemar's
exact test on the discordant seeds; secondary: Fisher's exact test, two-
sided; Wilson 95 % intervals on every rate (11/20 is 0.34 to 0.74).  At n = 20
per arm two independent rates need a gap of roughly 7 seeds to reach
p < 0.05 by Fisher (the round-1 validity critique's estimate: 11/20 vs 17/20
gives about 0.08), so the headline is descriptive with intervals.  Repetition
2 is the stability check (per-seed flips reported) and is never pooled with
repetition 1.  Per-layout rates (8, 7, 5 seeds) are reported without tests.
The +/-1-2 seed flip rate of identical code is the noise floor, not a
significance threshold.
TIME.  (t1) loop time = time_deltas[-1] on both arms; T's contains no
physical simulation time (headless teleport, no gravity), O's contains stepped
physics; both labelled.  (t2) process wall from each driver.  (t3) time to
first reward 1.0 for successes.  (t4) actions to goal at macro level (pick,
place, sense, look; each move folded into the action it serves, the final move
home into the goal) with O's raw dispatched count beside it.  (t5) planning
share on both arms: T's summed get_action spans; O's summed plan() spans plus
first-render and boxelization time.  ANYTIME FIGURE: cumulative success
fraction against elapsed loop time up to 900 s for both arms (T: the time at
solve_step; O: the time of the final action), so a reader can apply any
budget.  All times as mean +/- std and median with n, over all episodes
(censored at the cap, flagged) and success-only.

P4.7  RECORD FORMAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every decision, fix, measurement and abort during #P4 is a BF#N entry in
P4.14, in this shape:
  BF#N [OPEN|DONE|REJECTED|ABORTED]  <title>  (package Bk, date)
  WHAT: one paragraph.  EVIDENCE: file:line, run directory, numbers.
  BEFORE / AFTER (for any model, constant or harness change).
  CLAIM EFFECT: which thesis sentence(s) change and how (or "none").
  HONESTY CLASS: translation | model tweak | configuration | measurement |
  infrastructure | refactor | FORBIDDEN (then ABORTED with the reason).
  DECISION yyyy-mm-dd (who): the choice and the alternatives rejected.
  RESULT: outcome lines once run.
Commits: one per BF# entry where code changes, one-line subjects, on
tampura-bridge.  The bridge wiki page is updated per BF# that changes
architecture or the domain.  Agent review reports go under
archive/tampura_bridge_plan_2026-10-25/reviews/.
