D1  WHAT RUNS: our WHOLE pipeline (main loop, PDDLStream planner, streams,

    executor").  The thesis claims concern the whole method.

D2  HARNESS: our policy returns ONE action per episode, a schema named
    boxel_episode appended at runtime to env.problem_spec.action_schemas (no
    preconditions, identity effects_fn, OUR execute_fn).  Their rollout, their
    env.step, their Belief.update and their get_reward run untouched: our
    execute_fn builds the env adapter, takes the first render, boxelizes and
    runs the whole plan-act-sense loop (bounded by the episode wall cap), then
    returns (state, observation); their update applies the observation; their
    reward judges it; steps 1..19 return no-op (their no-op branch deep-copies
    the belief, so a reward reached at step 0 persists to rewards[-1] without
    any bridge write).  REJECTED: the archived no-op policy that mutated their
    SceneBelief in place (belief_bridge.py:44-58); it bypassed their
    observation channel and is where the forced success lived.

D3  SCORING CHANNEL: tampura_bridge/scoring.py ObservationAdapter builds the
    terminal observation from physical readback only:
    SceneObservation(camera_image=None, poses={}, grasp=G, grasp_body=S,
    conf=Q).  S is the store symbol s in env.sim_world.objects with
    store.get(s) == the body id that both finger pads contact with squeeze
    force >= 0.5 N (the harness's _verify_holding rule) and no constraint on
    that body, resolved with the same lookup their obs_from_camera_image uses
    (env.py:70-73); it is never chosen by name, and it is None when the pads
    hold nothing or a non-task body.  G is a store symbol of type grasp added
    once through store.add_typed(<our grasp record>, 'grasp') when S is not
    None.  Q is the real readback of the 7 arm joints, never a constant.
    camera_image and poses stay empty: the reward does not read them, and
    passing a render through their perception would pull true poses into the
    scoring path for no scoring effect.  The symbol lookup is the ONLY store
    read in scoring and is documented as such.  TAMPURA's own go-home reports
    at-home on a failed motion plan; ours cannot, so our arm is scored
    stricter on this point, which is disclosed, not imitated.

D4  CAMERA: a FIXED EXTERNAL camera at the pose camera_frame has when the arm
    is at DEFAULT_ARM_POS, with their intrinsics (640x480, fx=fy=525, vertical
    fov 49.1 deg), rendered with our TinyRenderer path, declared once in
    task_spec.py and used for every render (G20).  It is the thesis's sensor
    model unchanged (a fixed eye; sense calls no streams; methods.tex:170
    stays literally true) placed at the viewpoint of their generator's
    hidden check, so "hidden at t=0" means the same thing for both systems.
    When the arm is at home its hand is in frame exactly as in their check;
    when the arm is elsewhere it can occlude the eye, which the sense
    classifier already treats as a blocker.  REJECTED: (a) the physical wrist
    camera, because every non-sense render (census at planning time, pick
    re-observation, post-release checks, integrity checks; execution.py:343,
    720, 723, 821, 998, 1799, 1954, 2102; test_full_pipeline.py:1936, 2169)
    would come from an unplanned moving viewpoint, and the planning-time
    census would need either a phantom render from home or an unplanned move
    home, which is CB#120(g); (b) an external camera at any other pose,
    because it changes what "hidden" means relative to their generator.
    DISCLOSED: our eye observes after every action without moving the arm;
    theirs observes only on look, from 20 to 30 cm, and at initialize.

D5  ARM SELF-OCCLUSION (model tweak M1, CONDITIONAL, decided by B1(h) before
    any episode).  With the eye where the hand sits at home, the arm away from
    home may block the eye's view of the region it just uncovered.  The
    domain already handles the held object's self-occlusion with the
    handempty precondition on sense (added 2026-08-21 for exactly that
    reason).  M1 extends it: sense also requires (at_config q_clear) for a
    declared clearance configuration q_clear = DEFAULT_ARM_POS, which IS the
    planner's home_config symbol (one object, two roles).  Pre-registered
    rule: B1(h) renders, from the D4 eye, each cup's interior region grid
    with the arm at the post-place lift pose over each cell of the D8
    workspace window; if the worst blocked fraction exceeds
    SENSE_MARGINAL_BLOCKED_FRACTION (0.05), M1 is adopted; otherwise it is
    dropped.  The rule is decided on geometry, never on an episode outcome,
    and recorded in BF#.  Without M1 a blocked sense goes still_blocked,
    shrinks, strikes and after three strikes parks the region, which is
    exactly the failure M1 prevents.

D6  CONTAINMENT = INTERIOR REGIONS OF HOLLOW CLASSES (model tweak M2).  A
    category declared hollow in task_spec.py, with its cavity dimensions
    measured once in B1(b) from the YCB mesh (inner footprint = AABB minus
    wall thickness; cavity height), casts a SHADOW-typed belief region of kind
    interior over that cavity, created_by the hollow object and wired exactly
    like a lateral fragment (belief.add_shadow, shadow_occluder_map, census,
    corridor facts, sense over its grid).  The planner, the streams and the
    sense action are unchanged.  STATUS RULE (pre-registered, region-level,
    no per-object flag anywhere): an interior region is cast when a hollow
    body is REGISTERED from a render (initial boxelization, discovery,
    rediscovery), with status unknown, because the space under it has never
    been observed; it is NEVER cast at a place destination, because the robot
    places only into free cells, and free cells are observed space (D8 window
    restricts free space to the eye's view), so the cavity covers space
    already observed not to contain the target.  This is the same rule the
    thesis applies to lateral shadows (a relocated object casts no new
    shadow, methods.tex:157), now with an observational justification.
    REJECTED: a new region predicate (redundant with blocks_view_at +
    view_clear); lift-and-look or sense-while-holding (contradicts handempty
    and reopens scripted motion); interiors for every object (changes the
    thesis scenes: a 4 cm cube would be worth looking under a solid 13 cm
    cube); a "cups already moved are empty" flag (the archived per-cup
    bookkeeping, boxel_policy.py:352-353).  The generic variant (every object
    hollow) stays available behind a task-spec switch and is not used.

D7  GOAL AND AT-HOME: goal ('and', ('holding', die), ('at_config', q_home))
    with q_home the planner's home_config symbol; no new go_home action, the
    existing move action plans the motion home with the die as cargo.  The
    loop's at_config evaluator applies THEIR exact test (np.allclose on the
    7-joint readback, rtol = atol = 1e-4), so our loop neither stops before
    their reward can be 1.0 nor accepts a pose they would reject.  Executor
    change, general and inert on our scenes: a move whose target is a
    declared goal configuration ends with a final hold that converges to that
    tolerance or a bounded step cap (a move_robot_smooth parameter, B3, A/B on
    our scenes); the time counts inside loop time.  Bound against a livelock
    of near-zero moves: two consecutive moves to q_home that leave the
